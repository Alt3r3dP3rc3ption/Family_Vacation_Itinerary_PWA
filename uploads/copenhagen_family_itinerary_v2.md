# Copenhagen, Sweden & Minecraft Family Itinerary
**Dates:** June 12 – June 20, 2026  
**Basecamp:** Three-Story Apartment, Indre By  
[📍 Google Maps: Indre By](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Indre+By,+Copenhagen)

*A tailored trip balancing gaming highlights for Preston & Owen, stroller-friendly paths for Elliot, and deep cultural immersion for Michael & Carly.*

---

## ✈️ Friday, June 12: Departure
* **Evening:** Depart Raleigh (RDU) for the overnight transatlantic flight.
* **Transit Tip:** You will have a layover in Paris (CDG) before your final leg to Denmark. Ensure the boys have their electronics charged in their carry-ons for the layover.

## 🧳 Saturday, June 13: Arrival & The Indre By Reset
* **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at **Copenhagen Airport (CPH)**. 
    * [🌐 Official CPH Website](https://www.cph.dk/en) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Copenhagen+Airport+CPH)
    * *Transit Tip:* Take the driverless M2 Metro straight to **Kongens Nytorv** station [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kongens+Nytorv+Metro+Station+Copenhagen). Kids usually love sitting right at the front window to "drive" the train.
* **Check-in:** Drop bags at the Indre By apartment. With the three-story layout, everyone can claim a bed and decompress from the 12+ hours of travel.
* **Evening:** Keep it strictly low-friction. Step out into Indre By to grab a quick, casual dinner (pizza or burgers) within a five-minute walk.
* **Pro-Tip:** Prioritize an early bedtime to force the time zone adjustment. Do not push for sightseeing on day one.

## 🛡️ Sunday, June 14: Walkable Classics & Vikings
* **Morning:** Hit a local Indre By bakery for fresh pastries. Walk to the **National Museum of Denmark**.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://en.natmus.dk/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=National+Museum+of+Denmark+Copenhagen)
    * *Highlight:* Head straight for the Children’s Museum section—it features a playable Viking ship and historical dress-up gear that will keep Preston and Owen engaged while being tactile for Elliot.
* **Lunch:** Grab traditional smørrebrød (open-faced sandwiches) or familiar cafe food near the museum.
* **Afternoon:** Utilize the "reset button." Walk back to the apartment for a mid-afternoon breather.
* **Evening:** Take a relaxed stroll down the **Strøget** pedestrian street [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Stroget+Copenhagen).

## 👑 Monday, June 15: Castles & Canals
* **Morning:** Walk to **Rosenborg Castle** and explore **The King’s Garden (Kongens Have)**.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://www.kongernessamling.dk/en/rosenborg/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rosenborg+Castle+Copenhagen)
    * *Highlight:* The expansive, flat grounds are completely stroller-friendly and provide plenty of open grass for running off morning energy.
* **Lunch:** Head to **Torvehallerne**, Copenhagen's premier food market.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://torvehallernekbh.dk/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Torvehallerne+Copenhagen)
    * *Highlight:* It offers dozens of fast-casual stalls ranging from empanadas to fresh pasta, making it easy to satisfy everyone's distinct cravings simultaneously.
* **Afternoon:** Take a **Classic Canal Cruise** departing from nearby Nyhavn [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nyhavn+Copenhagen).
    * *Tip:* This provides a comprehensive tour of the city's architecture without requiring any walking—perfect for resting tired legs.

## 🎢 Tuesday, June 16: The Big Day at Tivoli & Astralis
* **Morning:** Enjoy a slower morning at the apartment.
* **Late Morning / Afternoon:** Walk over to **Tivoli Gardens**.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://www.tivoli.dk/en) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tivoli+Gardens+Copenhagen)
    * **For Preston & Owen:** Hit the **Astralis Nexus** gaming lounge ([🌐 Official Website](https://link.astralis.gg/m/nexus) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Astralis+Nexus+Tivoli+Copenhagen)) to jump on pro-level gaming PCs.
    * **For Carly & Elliot:** Enjoy the beautifully manicured gardens and gentle rides in the park while the older boys game.
* **Dinner:** Eat directly inside the park. The **Tivoli Food Hall** sits on the park's edge and offers diverse, quick-service options.

## ⛏️ Wednesday, June 17: The Epic Journey (Parallel Tracks)
* **Morning:** Head to **Copenhagen Central Station** [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Copenhagen+Central+Station) and board an Intercity train bound for Central Jutland. This 4-hour scenic ride serves as great built-in downtime.
* **Midday (The Split):**
    * **Team Minecraft (Michael, Preston, & Owen):** Disembark at the **Herning Messecenter station**. Walk right into the **Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue** at MCH Messecenter [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=MCH+Messecenter+Herning). Grab your Orbs of Interaction and spend 3-4 hours physically navigating the biome rooms.
    * **Team Culture & Chill (Carly & Elliot):** Take the local train one additional stop directly to **Birk Centerpark Station**. Enjoy a peaceful lunch at the **HEART Museum** cafe ([🌐 Official Website](https://www.heartmus.dk/en/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=HEART+Museum+Herning)). Then, explore the visually striking **Carl-Henning Pedersen & Else Alfelts Museum** ([🌐 Official Website](https://www.chpeamuseum.dk/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Carl-Henning+Pedersen+Museum+Herning)) and let Elliot stretch his legs in the massive, stroller-friendly **Geometrical Gardens (De Geometriske Haver)** [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=De+Geometriske+Haver+Herning).
* **Late Afternoon:** Carly and Elliot take the quick train back to Herning Central to reunite with you and the boys. Board the Intercity train back to Copenhagen together.
* **Evening:** Arrive in time for a late, relaxed dinner near the Indre By apartment.

## 🇸🇪 Thursday, June 18: Swedish Day Trip to Malmö
* **Morning:** Board the Øresundståg commuter train from a main station. The train crosses the massive **Øresund Bridge**, taking you directly into Sweden.
* **Late Morning:** Arrive at **Malmö Central Station** [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malmo+Central+Station). Walk over to **Malmöhus Castle (Malmöhus Slott)**.
    * [🌐 Official Info](https://malmo.se/Uppleva-och-gora/Konst-och-museer/Malmo-Museer/Besok-Malmo-Museer/Malmohus-slott.html) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malmohus+Castle+Malmo)
    * *Highlight:* It is an actual moat-surrounded fortress that also houses an aquarium and natural history exhibits inside.
* **Lunch:** Head to the picturesque **Lilla Torg** square [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lilla+Torg+Malmo) to secure a plate of traditional Swedish meatballs.
* **Afternoon:** Visit **Folkets Park**.
    * [🌐 Official Info](https://malmo.se/Uppleva-och-gora/Natur-och-parker/Folkets-park.html) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Folkets+Park+Malmo)
    * *Highlight:* It is the oldest public park in the world and features massive, creative playgrounds and plenty of room to run.
* **Evening:** Take the 40-minute train ride back to Denmark and grab dinner.

## 🧙‍♂️ Friday, June 19: Geek Culture & The Round Tower
* **Morning:** Visit the **Round Tower (Rundetårn)**.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://www.rundetaarn.dk/en/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Round+Tower+Copenhagen)
    * *Highlight:* Instead of stairs, a spiral equestrian ramp leads all the way to the top. It is incredibly easy to push a stroller up, and the older boys can run the incline.
* **Midday:** Pop into **Faraos Cigarer** on Skindergade.
    * [🌐 Official Website](https://www.faraos.dk/) | [📍 Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Faraos+Cigarer+Skindergade+Copenhagen)
    * *Highlight:* Let Preston and Owen browse Scandinavia’s premier geek and gaming store for a final souvenir.
* **Lunch:** Stop at a traditional Danish hot dog cart (pølsevogn) on the street—a classic, quick, and universally popular option.
* **Afternoon:** Final souvenir shopping and packing up the apartment.
* **Evening:** Farewell dinner in the neighborhood.

## 🛫 Saturday, June 20: Departure
* **Morning:** Final bakery run. Load up the bags and ride the M2 Metro back to the airport.
* **Flight:** Depart CPH, connect through Paris (CDG), and arrive back home in Raleigh (RDU).
