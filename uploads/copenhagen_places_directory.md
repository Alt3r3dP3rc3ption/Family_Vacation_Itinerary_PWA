# Comprehensive Denmark & Sweden Trip Directory

This directory categorizes all locations, attractions, and dining options discussed for the trip. Places are organized first by category (What they are) and then by location (Where they are), so you can cross-reference everything easily. 

---

## Part 1: Directory by Category

### 1. Attractions & Activities

**Amusement & Gaming**
* **Astralis Nexus:** Massive esports and gaming lounge with high-end PCs.
* **Experimentarium:** World-class, interactive science center. *(Discussed alternative)*
* **Extremezone VR & Action Park:** Indoor park with VR arena, trampolines, and ninja courses. *(Discussed alternative)*
* **Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue:** Life-sized, interactive biome rooms where players complete physical quests.
* **Tivoli Gardens:** Iconic amusement park featuring roller coasters, arcade games, and historic gardens.

**Museums & History**
* **Carl-Henning Pedersen & Else Alfelts Museum:** Art museum covered in blue ceramic tiles and surrounded by a moat.
* **HEART Museum of Contemporary Art:** Quiet, spacious architectural art museum designed by Steven Holl.
* **Malmöhus Castle (Malmöhus Slott):** Historic moat-surrounded fortress housing an aquarium and natural history exhibits.
* **National Museum of Denmark:** National history museum featuring an interactive Children’s Museum with a playable Viking ship.
* **Rosenborg Castle:** 17th-century renaissance castle housing the crown jewels.
* **Round Tower (Rundetårn):** Historic 17th-century tower featuring a stroller-friendly spiral equestrian ramp to the top.

**Parks, Nature & Outdoors**
* **Copenhagen Zoo:** Highly stroller-friendly zoo featuring an Arctic Ring and elephant enclosures.
* **Folkets Park:** The oldest public park in the world, featuring massive creative playgrounds.
* **Frederiksberg Gardens:** Massive suburban green space with water features and relaxed pathways.
* **Geometrical Gardens (De Geometriske Haver):** A landscape park featuring intricate hedge mazes.
* **The King’s Garden (Kongens Have):** Expansive, flat, and highly stroller-friendly grounds surrounding Rosenborg Castle.

**Shopping & Leisure**
* **Classic Canal Cruise:** Guided boat tours departing from Nyhavn or Gammel Strand.
* **Faraos Cigarer:** Scandinavia’s premier geek, gaming, and pop-culture store.
* **Strøget:** One of Europe's longest pedestrian shopping streets.

---

### 2. Food & Dining

**Cafes & Quick Bites**
* **Danish Hot Dog Carts (Pølsevogn):** Traditional street food carts found throughout the city.
* **HEART Museum Cafe:** Quiet, high-quality lunch cafe perfect for decompressing.
* **Local Indre By Bakeries/Pizzerias/Burger Joints:** Quick, zero-friction street food surrounding the apartment.

**Food Halls & Markets**
* **Tivoli Food Hall:** Diverse, quick-service food options located on the edge of Tivoli Gardens.
* **Torvehallerne:** Copenhagen's premier food market offering dozens of distinct fast-casual stalls.

**Restaurants & Squares**
* **Lilla Torg:** Picturesque historic square known for restaurants serving traditional Swedish meatballs.
* **Smørrebrød Cafes:** Traditional Danish open-faced sandwich spots (recommended near the National Museum).

---

### 3. Transit Hubs & Infrastructure

* **Birk Centerpark Station:** Local train stop for the Herning art and culture district.
* **Copenhagen Airport (CPH):** Main international airport.
* **Copenhagen Central Station:** Main hub for regional and Intercity trains.
* **Herning Messecenter Station:** Direct train stop for the MCH Messecenter (Minecraft Experience).
* **Kongens Nytorv Metro Station:** Automated M2 line station near the Indre By apartment.
* **Malmö Central Station:** Main transit hub for arriving in Sweden.
* **Øresund Bridge:** The massive engineering marvel connecting Denmark and Sweden by train.

---

### 4. Accommodations

* **Three-Story Apartment (Indre By):** The primary basecamp. Offers maximum sleeping space and zero-friction food access.
* **Jægersborggade Apartment (Nørrebro):** *(Discussed Alternative)* Not selected due to transit logistics.
* **Valby Penthouse (Valby):** *(Discussed Alternative)* Highly rated, but adds commuter friction with kids.

## Part 2: Directory by Location

### 1. Copenhagen: Indre By (City Center)
*This is your home base, meaning all these locations are highly walkable or easily accessible from the apartment.*
* **Accommodations:** Three-Story Apartment
* **Attractions:** Astralis Nexus, Faraos Cigarer, National Museum of Denmark, Rosenborg Castle, Round Tower (Rundetårn), Strøget, The King’s Garden (Kongens Have), Tivoli Gardens
* **Dining:** Indre By Bakeries/Pizzas/Burgers, Smørrebrød Cafes, Tivoli Food Hall, Torvehallerne
* **Transit/Infrastructure:** Classic Canal Cruise (Nyhavn/Gammel Strand), Kongens Nytorv Metro Station, Copenhagen Central Station

### 2. Copenhagen: Frederiksberg / Valby / Hellerup (Suburbs)
*Slightly outside the center; requires a short bus, Metro, or commuter train ride.*
* **Attractions:** Copenhagen Zoo, Experimentarium (Hellerup), Frederiksberg Gardens
* **Accommodations:** Valby Penthouse *(Alternative)*

### 3. Herning (Central Jutland)
*A 4-hour scenic Intercity train ride from Copenhagen.*
* **Attractions (Messecenter):** Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue
* **Attractions (Birk Centerpark):** Carl-Henning Pedersen & Else Alfelts Museum, Geometrical Gardens (De Geometriske Haver), HEART Museum of Contemporary Art
* **Dining:** HEART Museum Cafe
* **Transit:** Birk Centerpark Station, Herning Messecenter Station

### 4. Malmö (Sweden)
*A 40-minute commuter train ride across the Øresund Bridge.*
* **Attractions:** Extremezone VR & Action Park, Folkets Park, Malmöhus Castle (Malmöhus Slott)
* **Dining:** Lilla Torg (Swedish Meatballs)
* **Transit:** Malmö Central Station, Øresund Bridge
